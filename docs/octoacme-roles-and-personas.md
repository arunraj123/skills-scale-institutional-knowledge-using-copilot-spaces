# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads ensure releases meet quality standards and that features satisfy acceptance criteria before production deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and acceptance criteria validation approach
- Manage manual and automated test planning
- Identify, document, and triage defects with clear reproduction details
- Partner with Developers on testability and regression prevention
- Validate release readiness and support rollout confidence

### Goals
- Deliver reliable features with minimal defects
- Catch issues earlier in the delivery cycle
- Improve test coverage and quality visibility across teams

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and Definition of Done reviews
- Bug triage and release readiness check-ins
- QA signoff discussions before deployment

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to ensure testability and timely bug fixes
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and release readiness
- Coordinates with Project Managers to flag risk, quality issues, and bottlenecks
- Supports Sponsor and Stakeholder reporting with validation evidence

---

## Technical Lead / Solution Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Solution Architects provide technical vision, standards, and decision-making support for product and platform work.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design and architectural decisions
- Review system trade-offs and long-term maintainability
- Mentor engineers and promote engineering best practices
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and mitigation options
- Ensure solutions remain scalable, secure, and maintainable

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Reduce rework, complexity, and technical debt
- Support team growth and consistent engineering standards

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions and architecture discussions
- Technical risk reviews and planning workshops
- Cross-team dependency alignment and escalation discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers to shape implementation choices and review technical execution
- Works with Product Managers and Project Managers on feasibility, sequencing, and risk
- Coordinates with Security / Compliance roles to ensure secure-by-default implementation
- Provides decision support to Sponsors and Stakeholders on technical trade-offs

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Agile Coaches help teams improve delivery flow, remove friction, and run effective agile practices with clear accountability.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Help the team resolve impediments and improve workflow
- Support agile practices while adapting to team maturity and context
- Promote transparency, collaboration, and continuous improvement
- Guide healthy rituals and decision-making without owning delivery outcomes

### Goals
- Improve delivery predictability and team effectiveness
- Reduce process friction and unnecessary coordination overhead
- Strengthen continuous learning and team health

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups, planning sessions, and retrospectives
- Team health and delivery flow discussions
- Coaching conversations with Project Managers and team leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to keep delivery plans and schedules realistic
- Supports Product Managers by improving backlog readiness and team flow
- Helps Developers work more effectively through impediment removal
- Provides the team with a structured way to surface blockers to Sponsors and stakeholders when needed

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors and executive stakeholders provide business context, resources, and strategic direction for the project.

### Responsibilities
- Approve the business case and overall project direction
- Allocate budget, staffing, and executive support
- Make priority, funding, and go/no-go decisions
- Review milestones, risk, and strategic impact
- Support escalation for high-impact blockers or changes

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers strategic value
- Align execution with business priorities
- Maintain sponsorship and accountability for outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews, executive updates, and steering meetings
- Escalation calls for major risks or business impact
- Funding and prioritization decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to validate outcomes and priority
- Receives regular updates from Project Managers and PMO-style delivery leads
- Provides decision support to Technical Leads and Developers through resource and priority trade-offs
- Engages with Security and Compliance roles when regulatory or risk-related decisions are required

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure that projects meet policy, privacy, and security requirements while managing risk across the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review security requirements and compliance obligations
- Assess risks, threat models, and control gaps
- Support secure design and implementation reviews
- Coordinate incident response and escalation for security issues
- Maintain awareness of privacy, governance, and operational controls

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Protect customer trust and company data
- Enable safe and compliant delivery

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings and architecture checkpoints
- Risk assessments and incident response updates
- Compliance checkpoints tied to release or deployment readiness

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Technical Leads and Developers to embed secure patterns and guardrails
- Provides risk context to Project Managers and Product Managers for planning and escalation
- Supports Sponsors and Stakeholders with policy and risk visibility
- Works with QA / Testing Leads to validate release readiness from a security and compliance perspective

---

## Cross-Functional Team Member

### Role Summary
Cross-functional team members contribute specialized expertise from adjacent disciplines, helping the team deliver complete value across product, delivery, and operations.

### Responsibilities
- Contribute domain, operational, or technical expertise to project work
- Support collaboration across business, engineering, and support functions
- Participate in planning, testing, implementation, and rollout activities
- Surface dependencies and operational impacts early
- Help ensure the solution is usable, supportable, and sustainable

### Goals
- Improve delivery quality through broader team capability
- Reduce handoff gaps and cross-team rework
- Support a complete end-to-end solution experience

### Typical Communication
- Team meetings, dependency reviews, and operational handoff conversations
- Support and readiness planning for rollouts or launches
- Cross-functional issue and risk reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and Technical Leads to support implementation and validation
- Collaborates with Product Managers and Project Managers on scope, dependencies, and readiness
- Supports QA and Security roles with operational or domain expertise
- Helps Sponsors and Stakeholders understand delivery trade-offs and real-world impact
